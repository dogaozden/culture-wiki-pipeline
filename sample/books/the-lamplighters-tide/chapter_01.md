# Chapter One: The Tide Bell

Marrow Quill had kept the lamp on Saltgrave Point for eleven winters, and in all that time nobody had asked her what the lamp was for.

On the morning the Tide Bell rang by itself, a barge called the Patient Heron came up the estuary with no crew on deck. Its steering was handled by a small, polite machine named Oddment, which introduced itself from a speaker the size of a teacup.

"I am sorry to arrive unannounced," said Oddment. "My passenger is asleep and I did not want to wake anyone with a formal request."

Marrow lit the lamp anyway. It was what she did when she was unsure.
