# Chapter Two: Saltgrave Point

Saltgrave Point was a spit of grey stone at the mouth of the Orrin estuary, where the river gave up and became the sea.

The passenger of the Patient Heron turned out to be Tamsin Verrow, a cartographer who drew maps of places that did not exist yet. She carried a satchel of blank charts and a folding stool, and she thanked Marrow for the tea in a way that sounded like an apology.

"The Tide Bell is older than the town," said Marrow. "The town only grew up around it to have something to complain about."

Oddment translated the weather for them. Rain by dusk, it said, and a long fog after.
